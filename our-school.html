Our School

Limitless Schools is the next evolution in inclusive, whole-child learning — a place where children with autism feel seen, heard, and understood.

Welcome to Limitless

Every child is full of potential.

At Limitless Schools, we believe that every child is full of potential — and that unlocking that potential begins with connection. Founded by Monica G. Osgood, a trailblazer in developmental, relationship-based education, Limitless Schools is the next evolution in inclusive, whole-child learning environments. With over 30 years of experience pioneering programs rooted in the DIR® model, Monica brings a deep understanding of neurodiversity, human development, and the power of meaningful relationships to this groundbreaking new school.

Our mission is simple yet transformative: to nurture happy, secure, social, and creative learners by building strong, respectful relationships and embracing each child's unique profile. We understand that real progress begins with trust — and for many children, especially those who experience the world in a disorganized or overwhelming way, that trust requires patience, empathy, and consistency. At Limitless, we create environments where children feel seen, heard, and understood.

Mission & Vision

We see the gifts waiting to be discovered.

Limitless Schools recognizes that autism is not an intellectual disorder — it is a sensory-processing difference. Many of our students are bright, insightful, and full of gifts waiting to be discovered. Through a whole-body, interdisciplinary approach to learning, we support development from the ground up: self-regulation, engagement, communication, emotional thinking, and abstract reasoning.

Our curriculum is designed not only to meet academic goals but to support each child's social, emotional, and sensory growth. Families are a vital part of the journey. We work alongside parents and caregivers, helping restore joy in their relationships with their children and partnering with them to create lasting, meaningful progress.

"There are no ceilings — only new paths to explore."
