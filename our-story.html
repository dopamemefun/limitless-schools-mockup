Mission & Vision

We see the gifts waiting to be discovered.

Our mission is simple yet transformative: to nurture happy, secure, social, and creative learners by building strong, respectful relationships and embracing each child's unique profile. Real progress begins with trust — and for many children, that trust requires patience, empathy, and consistency.

Limitless Schools recognizes that autism is not an intellectual disorder — it is a sensory-processing difference. Many of our students are bright, insightful, and full of gifts waiting to be discovered.

"There are no ceilings — only new paths to explore."

The Limitless Way

How we do what we do

The Limitless Way is a whole-body, interdisciplinary approach to learning — supporting development from the ground up.

Relationships first

Connection comes before instruction. Children learn from the people they trust.

Every profile honored

Each child's sensory and developmental profile shapes how we teach — not the other way around.

Whole-child growth

Self-regulation, engagement, communication, emotional thinking, and abstract reasoning — built step by step.

Families as partners

We help restore joy in family relationships and partner with caregivers for lasting progress.

The Limitless Lens

How we see every child

The Limitless Lens is our way of looking at children: through possibility, not deficit.

Where others might see behaviors to manage, we look for the message behind them. Where others see limits, we look for the next path to explore. It's a lens we teach, practice, and share with families every day.

Led by a DIR® pioneer.

Founder and Executive Director Monica G. Osgood has spent over 30 years pioneering developmental, relationship-based programs — creating the first public-school program using the DIR® model in 1998 and joining ICDL's senior faculty in 2000.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions families ask us

Draft answers — to be reviewed and confirmed by the school before launch.
Who does Limitless Schools serve?

Limitless is a New Jersey state-approved private school for students with disabilities, serving children with autism (ages shown as 3–21, to be confirmed) in Parsippany, NJ.

What is the DIR® model?

DIR® stands for Developmental, Individual-differences, Relationship-based. It's an approach that builds learning on trust, connection, and each child's unique profile. Read more about our developmental approach.

How does my child enroll?

Families can begin with a parent inquiry, and school districts can start a district referral. The best first step is usually a visit — schedule one here or call our tour line at 862-219-3979.

Is Limitless a nonprofit?

Yes — Limitless Schools is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. You can support our mission on our Support Limitless page.

How can I see the school in person?

Schedule a visit — we'd love to show you around and introduce the people who will know your child by name.
