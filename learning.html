Learning in Action

Learning that starts with joy.

At Limitless, learning is active, playful, and relationship-driven. Teachers follow each child's interests and ideas — turning play into engagement, engagement into communication, and communication into real academic growth.

Photos and stories from our classrooms will appear here regularly, so families and visitors can see learning as it happens.

Curriculum Overview

A full course of study, made accessible

Academic goals and developmental goals travel together — every subject is a chance to build connection, communication, and confidence.

Core academics

  • English & Math
  • Science & Social Studies
  • Computer Skills & Economics

Arts & electives

  • Fine Arts
  • Music & movement
  • Electives that follow student interests

Developmental goals

  • Self-regulation & engagement
  • Communication & language
  • Emotional thinking & reasoning
Classrooms

Small, intentional, and calm.

Our classrooms are designed around the children in them — small groups, predictable routines, and sensory-aware spaces where students can regulate, engage, and do their best learning.

Individual classroom pages, with photos and updates from each room, are planned for a future phase of this site.

Student Life

More than a school day

Music, movement, imaginative play, celebrations, and friendships — the moments between lessons matter just as much.

Community Learning

The community is our classroom, too.

Real-world experiences — outings, community partners, and guest visitors — help students practice skills where they matter most.

Stories from our community learning adventures will be shared on the Community page.
