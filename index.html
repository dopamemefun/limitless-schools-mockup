Welcome to Limitless

Potential begins with connection.

We nurture happy, secure, social, and creative learners by building strong, respectful relationships and embracing each child's unique profile.

Founded by Monica G. Osgood, a trailblazer in developmental, relationship-based education, Limitless is the next evolution in inclusive, whole-child learning. We understand that real progress begins with trust — and we create environments where children feel seen, heard, and understood.

Our approach

A whole-child approach to learning

Academics meet social, emotional, and sensory growth — built around each student, not the other way around.

Academics

A full course of study:

  • English & Math
  • Science & Social Studies
  • Computer Skills & Economics
  • Fine Arts & Electives

The DIR® Model

Relationships are the driving force of growth. We connect first, then build self-regulation, communication, and reasoning from there.

Whole-child growth

  • Self-regulation & engagement
  • Communication & language
  • Emotional thinking
  • Abstract reasoning

Family partnership

We work alongside parents and caregivers — helping restore joy in their relationships and creating lasting progress together.

Why families choose us

The first truly appropriate placement.

Autism is not an intellectual disorder — it is a sensory-processing difference. Many of our students are bright, insightful, and full of gifts waiting to be discovered.

  • Built on the DIR® model — Developmental, Individual-differences, Relationship-based.
  • Small, intentional classrooms — we adapt to each child.
  • Founded by a DIR® pioneer with 30+ years' experience.
