Our Exceptional Team

Committed to nurturing the potential of every child, with a focus on inclusive learning environments for students with diverse needs.

Executive Director

Monica G. Osgood

A behavioral consultant and therapist specializing in developmental approaches for children with autism. Monica founded the Developmental Center for Children and Families and serves as a Board Member for the Profectum Foundation and Celebrate the Individual (CTI).

With over 30 years of experience, she created the first public-school program using the DIR® model in 1998, and was the first educator to join ICDL's senior faculty in 2000.
